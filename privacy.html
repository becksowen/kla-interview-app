Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Knowledge Learning Academy ("we", "us", or "our") operates the KLA Video Interview System at https://kla-interview.netlify.app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our video interview application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the KLA Video Interview System, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal identification: Your full name as entered at the start of the interview
  • Video recording: Your video and audio responses to interview questions
  • Program information: The program you are applying to at Knowledge Learning Academy
  • Email address: Provided by the admission advisor to send your interview invitation

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To conduct and record your admissions video interview
  • To allow admission advisors at Knowledge Learning Academy to review your interview responses
  • To assess your communication skills and suitability for the program you are applying to
  • To notify our admission team when your interview is complete

3. Video Storage

Your video interview recording is stored securely in Google Drive, accessible only to authorised staff at Knowledge Learning Academy. Videos are stored on Google's secure servers and are subject to Google's own privacy and security policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your interview recording and personal information are only accessible to:

  • Admission advisors and authorised staff at Knowledge Learning Academy
  • Google LLC (for secure video storage via Google Drive)

5. Data Retention

Your video interview recording and associated personal information will be retained for the duration of your application process. Once a decision has been made on your application, recordings may be deleted at the discretion of Knowledge Learning Academy.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information held by us
  • Request deletion of your video recording and personal data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us

7. Google OAuth

This application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to securely upload your video interview to Google Drive. We only request access to Google Drive for the purpose of storing your interview recording. We do not access any other Google services or data.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Video recordings are transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption.
